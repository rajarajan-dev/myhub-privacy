# MyHub Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

MyHub is a personal productivity app for managing todos, notes, and links. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information stored on your device

Todos, notes, links, and app preferences are stored in the app's local SQLite database on your device. MyHub does not require you to create an account to use these features. The developer does not receive this locally stored content.

## Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect a Google account, MyHub uses Google Sign-In to obtain the permission needed to access the app's private Google Drive appDataFolder. The app may display your Google account name and email address in its settings. When you start a backup or restore, the backup file contains your todos, notes, links, and backup metadata. It is compressed and stored in your Google Drive app data area. The backup is available to MyHub through the Google Drive API and is not sent to a MyHub-operated server. Google's handling of your account and Drive data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Google Drive connection and backup are optional. You can disconnect Google Drive from MyHub settings. Disconnecting removes the locally stored account connection; it does not delete an existing backup from Google Drive. Manage or delete the app's Drive data through your Google Account settings.

## Export and import

If you export a backup, MyHub creates a file on your device and presents the operating system's share or save interface. You choose whether and where to share or save that file. MyHub does not receive a copy. Imported files are read to validate and restore their MyHub data to the local database.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

MyHub does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not operate a service that collects your app content or account information.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app and its local data. You control backups stored in Google Drive and can delete them from your Google Account. Exported copies are controlled by the destination you selected.

## Children's privacy

MyHub is a general-purpose productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy URL listed on the App Store.
